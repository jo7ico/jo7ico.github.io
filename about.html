Who Jo7ico is

Jo7ico is a student, web developer & designer, creator, and music producer that develop digital and beautifully designed projects and mainly focuses on making experiences glow and feel premium, effortless, and beautiful. Jo7ico is also a music producer and multimedia editor in a band/music group called DEM1AN.

Image by Abhishek Kumar Biswas from Pixabay

What Jo7ico does

Jo7ico designs, produces, and develops projects (especially websites) with psychology, purpose, and innovation. For him personally, every single detail, even small ones people probably won't notice, matters. Everything is part of a design. Everything is a part of a project. Everything is a part of a work. You can't leave things behind and call it complete. That's incomplete. He mainly focus on two things: Clean, smooth, and thoughtful design and make digital experiences that stand out from others and human-centered.

Why it matters

Designs need more ideas. More creativity. More expression. More style. And this is what distinguishes human work and works by Artificial Intelligence.

Jo7ico grew up with a deep fascination for both technology and music and started his journey from music production. He started producing his own music using his piano at grade 3. At grade 4, he started producing music with Beepbox, which was a simple online DAW. In middle school, he started producing music with Garageband, making dozens and dozens of songs. He was also fascinated by how websites and applications were made, so in his second year of middle school, he also started learning how to code by himself through cheat sheets and Youtube tutorials (no classes). Through coding simple websites, he developed his own style of web design, which was simple, clean, and colorful with around 3 colors. He often used white transparent (not fully) backgrounds for containers and used colors such as darkslateblue, gold, and rosybrown. He also loved adding smooth transitions and animation to make it even more user friendly. This design is what you see in Jo7ico's projects today.
