Jo7ico is Now a DEM1AN Member

May 8, 2026

Suzhou —— Jo7ico has joined the band/music group of DEM1AN as a multimedia editor, music producer, and web developer after months of cooperation between Jo7ico and the DEM1AN group.


DEM1AN is a music and production group that focuses on creating music and content that is unique and original, with a focus on creativity and innovation. Jo7ico will be working closely with the group to create new music, music videos, and other content that will be released in the future.


Jo7ico is currently developing the official website of DEM1AN. Stay tuned!.


Jo7ico and the music group DEM1AN has been working together for a while now and soon, Jo7ico joined the group, helping to make DEM1AN's second release: Hikikomori.
